Control Systems Virtual Laboratory

Welcome to the Control Systems Virtual Laboratory developed by the Department of Electronics and Instrumentation Engineering, SRM Institute of Science and Technology. This platform enables students to perform virtual experiments, visualize system responses, analyze control system performance, and strengthen their understanding of core control engineering concepts.

Laboratory Objective

The primary objective of this virtual laboratory is to provide an interactive learning environment where students can explore fundamental and advanced concepts of Control Systems without requiring physical laboratory equipment. The laboratory bridges theoretical knowledge and practical understanding through simulation-based experiments.

Transfer Function Analysis

Study mathematical models of dynamic systems and their representations using transfer functions.

Time Response Analysis

Investigate transient and steady-state responses of first-order and higher-order systems.

Stability Analysis

Evaluate system stability using characteristic equations and standard stability criteria.

Frequency Response

Analyze Bode plots, Nyquist plots and frequency-domain characteristics of control systems.

Controller Design

Design and evaluate P, PI, PD and PID controllers for improved system performance.

Self Assessment

Reinforce learning through quizzes, exercises and concept-based evaluation.
