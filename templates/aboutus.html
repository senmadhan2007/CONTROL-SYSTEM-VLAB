About the Department

The Department of Electronics and Instrumentation Engineering was established in the academic year 2007–08 with the vision of developing highly skilled engineers capable of addressing the challenges of modern automation, instrumentation, process control, robotics, and intelligent manufacturing systems.

The department has grown into a centre of excellence in engineering education by combining strong academic foundations with practical learning experiences. Students are encouraged to participate in innovation, research, industrial projects, and interdisciplinary activities that enhance their professional and technical competencies.

The department is accredited by the IET Accreditation Board (UK) and recognized by the IASC Sector Skill Council, affiliated with the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship, Government of India.

Vision

The department is committed to enabling students to experience an uncompromised educational journey that facilitates successful careers and excellence in the field of Electronics and Instrumentation Engineering while maintaining strong social and ethical values.

Mission

  • To create a scholarly and professional environment.
  • To provide world-class teaching resources and laboratory facilities.
  • To develop innovative, dynamic, and flexible academic programs.
  • To foster research and development for the benefit of society.
